# Roadmap: what is pending, in version order

Written 2026-10-09 after comparing the app with TrueProfit, BeProfit, Lifetimely by AMP, MerchantFlow, PodVector, Triple Whale (ProfitFly could not be found on the Shopify App Store, so it is not compared). Competitor facts come from their public listings and vendor pages and may be out of date; check the current listings before pricing decisions.

Effort: S under a week, M one to three weeks, L a month or more. "Owner" means something only the owner can do (accounts, approvals, money).

## What the app already does (not in the list below)

Profit engine (cash view, half-up, decimal safe, frozen cost history) · Overview, Profit, Products, Orders, Marketing, Expenses, Reports, Settings · profit and loss, product, order, marketing and expense reports as CSV and PDF · scheduled email reports · operating expenses · manual advertising spend · data completeness and issues · profit opportunities (losses, refunds, thin margins) · revenue by sales channel · plan limits and Shopify billing code (off by default) · security, backups, monitoring, secret scanning · 20% smaller responsive UI.

## V1: launch version (the smallest product merchants will pay for)

Ordered by importance. The first six decide whether the profit figure is right and whether the app can be approved and sold.

1. **Ads integration: Meta and Google Ads (spend and reported sales).** Built as a daily spend sync (06:00 UTC). The merchant pastes an ad account id and access token; spend, blended ROAS and POAS show next to profit. Campaign-to-order matching is later. Google also needs `GOOGLE_ADS_DEVELOPER_TOKEN` on the worker. TikTok stays manual.
2. **Actual payment fees from Shopify Payments.** Built. Fees Shopify reports on the order, and Shopify Payments balance-transaction fees when `read_shopify_payments_accounts` and `read_shopify_payments_payouts` are granted, are actual. Gateway rules stay a labeled estimate. Orders still on an estimate are listed under Orders, Fees estimated.
3. **Shipping cost rules by zone, country and weight, plus CSV import.** Built. A country or weight band beats a catch-all, per kilogram needs a product weight, and CSV import replaces the active rules. Destination country is the two-letter code only. Recalculate existing orders after the next product and order sync so weights and countries are filled in.
4. **Product cost CSV import and export, and bulk edit** (by product, variant, collection, vendor). Built. Import matches a variant id, then a SKU, and a blank cost leaves that product alone. Collection bulk edit uses collection titles from the next product sync.
5. **Live billing and plans.** Choose managed pricing or the Billing API, approve prices, switch `BILLING_MODE` on, trial, upgrade and downgrade tested. _S to M. Owner: prices._
6. **Deployment and App Store readiness (Phase 14).** HTTPS hosting, monitoring alerts, backups restored, privacy policy, support contact, listing text and screenshots, `read_all_orders` approval or a 60-day history, performance and accessibility pass, rotate the leaked Shopify secret. _M. Owner: policy, support address, secret rotation._
7. **Exchange rates for any currency.** _Built: daily ECB rates stored in the database, with history; AED stays on its fixed peg; shown as "estimated" when a rate is more than 7 days old. Until those rates are stored, only USD and AED convert, and the overview says so. Ad spend in another currency uses the same rates. Settings, Currency shows the store currency, that rate source, the latest rate date, the rounding rule, and the shop time zone. The currency and time zone come from Shopify and are not edited here._
8. **Email delivery in production** (SMTP or a mail service) for scheduled reports and alerts. Code is done. _S. Owner: provide the account._
9. **Alerts by email.** _Built: Settings, Alerts, "Email alerts" (up to 5 addresses). Warnings and serious findings of the daily profit check and stale-data notices are emailed in one message per run, never twice. Needs the mail account (item 8) to actually deliver._
10. **Real-data testing and a UI polish pass.** Reconcile against the dev store passed (`pnpm sync:verify`, `pnpm profit:verify`): 81 orders in the 90-day window match Shopify, and the profit ledger matches the approved examples. One older order stays stored after it ages out of the window. UI polish is still open. _M._

### V1 review items (products, orders, expenses, settings, reports, sync)

Built: fee source per order (Reported, Estimated from rule, Missing) with filters; refund lines beyond 25 fetched, and orders still incomplete flagged; order detail links to the Settings screen that fixes a missing cost, fee rule or shipping rule; partial sync lists what was skipped with a retry; rule coverage and a check before saving a fee or shipping rule; shipping data gaps (no country, no weight); recalculation result (orders changed, now complete, profit before and after); CSV import problem list with a downloadable summary; export audit block and fee and shipping source labels; expenses net-profit banner and monthly profit and loss; products missing-cost banner and an explanation of what stays unallocated; collection bulk edit verified with tests; reconciliation against Shopify run (`pnpm sync:verify`, `pnpm profit:verify`); fresh-database migration run; privacy and support pages, listing text and the history decision (`docs/decisions/0002-order-history-window.md`).

Not built (needs the owner): billing prices, hosting and monitoring, `read_all_orders` approval (request text: `docs/operations/read-all-orders-request.md`), rotating the leaked Shopify secret, a domain mailbox for launch (SMTP works today with a test Gmail account), company name and support email for the privacy page, ad-platform developer accounts. Expense category class and the expense default are done (migrations 0025 and 0026). Not seen in a browser yet: the expense defaults card, the Expenses date fields and the product Refund history section.

### Combined V1: milestone progress

- **M1 done (financial definitions):** contribution profit = revenue - product cost - payment fees - shipping cost; advertising is an operating cost and is no longer inside it. Estimated operating profit = contribution profit - advertising - other operating expenses (field `estimatedOperatingProfit`, margin `operatingMargin`, basis `operatingProfitBasis`; expenses summary `operatingProfit` and `operatingProfitStatus`). Overview Total costs = product + shipping + fees, with advertising and other operating expenses listed apart. The old `contributionBeforeAds`, `estimatedNetProfit`, `netProfitBasis` and the comparison `basis` fields are gone; no screen says "net profit". Definitions: `docs/finance/figures.md`.
- **M2 done (Refund report and Missing cost report):** both are reports 6 and 7 of the seven: preview with search, filter and sort, CSV and PDF with the audit block, scheduled emails, totals equal to the Overview to the cent. Migration `0024_report_kinds_refund_missing` widens the kind lists on exports and schedules. Migration numbers: Cursor's expenses migration is now `0025`, and my performance migration will be `0026`.
- **M6 done (performance, measured):** `pnpm test:load` runs 50,000 synthetic orders through the real API. Overview p95 under 1 s, Orders list p95 under 200 ms; the Products list was the slow one (4.2 s) and is now kept per shop and period until an order is recalculated (p95 about 0.5 s warm, 4.2 s for the first request at 50,000 orders in the period). The order-list rewrite the plan expected was not needed: it measured fast. Numbers, method and limits: `docs/operations/performance.md`.
- **M3 done (Orders and Products completeness):** Orders list shows gross sales, discounts, refunds, net sales, payment and fulfillment status; order detail adds Shipping (country, charged, cost with status), Payments and fees (reported fee per transaction) and SKU and unit price/cost per line; Products has a By product / By variant and SKU view (variants add up to the product), SKU search, and a product detail with variants, daily trend, cost history and missing-cost banner. Tests: orders and products completeness in `profitability.integration.test.ts`.
- **Ad platform connectors done (V2):** Meta, Google and TikTok connect by signing in (Connect, choose accounts, Connected, Disconnect), tokens stored encrypted and renewed before each daily import, TikTok importer added. Migration `0027_ad_oauth_connections`. Tested with fake platforms only; the first real connection needs the owner's developer apps (`docs/operations/ad-platforms.md`). Marketing is now one page (KPI cards, a card per platform with Connect or its spend, chart, channels, campaigns, manual entry; no tabs) and the Overview has a Marketing card (ad spend, profit after ads, blended return, or a Connect prompt). First-run: the checklist has an optional "Connect your ad platforms" step, the setup card on the Overview shows the next two steps (and can be dismissed once only optional ones remain), and connecting a platform or pressing "Refresh spend now" queues an import for that shop at once (`enqueueAdSync`). The Overview also has an Expenses card (operating expenses, estimated operating profit, operating margin; a prompt when none are set up), and the sidebar lists Overview once, as the app home (`rel="home"`). Products: a profit trend line in every row and a "What is missing, and how to improve it" section (missing costs, estimated costs, losses, high refunds, variants without SKU, each with how to fix it and the biggest products). Every page has a notifications bell with the unread count and an "open in a new tab" icon (`TopBar`). Plan screen: orders this month (information only), an "In every plan" list, and an Ask us link. Plans now include orders per month (Free 50, Growth 500, Pro 2,000, draft) with $0.10 per extra order on paid plans, shown as a meter on the Plan screen (an estimate: nothing is charged until paid plans open; usage charges through Shopify are not wired). The top row of every page is one taller strip: setup notice on the left, bell and open-in-new-tab on the right; the red unread-notifications card is removed. Product costs are set on the Products screen only ("Set product costs": one by one, in bulk, CSV import and export); the Settings tab is gone and an old link shows a pointer. The top-right icons are larger bordered buttons. Products is one view again: the table first, Set product costs / Import costs (CSV) / Download template in the toolbar and a Set cost link on each row open the cost tools in a panel above the table; the "what is missing" findings are one short row each below it; the top-right icons stay put on every tab. Top selling and Top returned product cards: on the Products screen (above the table, following its period) and on the Overview (own period filter); the products list can be sorted by refunds. USD is the only currency (`docs/decisions/0003-usd-only.md`): a store in another currency is converted to USD before profit is worked out (rate of each day, never 1 to 1; an order with no rate is left out with a reason), no display-currency switching, costs and CSV in USD only. Products page redesigned: four headline cards, the table first, Bulk Product Cost dialog (import, bulk edit, export) and a Set cost dialog per product, top selling and returned, what needs attention (top 3), reconciliation collapsed; costs shown as positive money spent.
- **Cursor prompt audit (`docs/curosrprompt.md`):** M5, M3 and M4 are done. Added in the audit: expense date-range filter (From and To), an invalid calendar date is now a 400 instead of a 500 (shared `isoDate`), and the cost-class and double-counting rule is written in `docs/architecture/expenses-advertising.md`. Also built: expense defaults in Settings (what a new expense starts as; migration `0026_expense_default_settings`, audited, used by the Add expense form). Product detail also has a Refund history section.
- **LTV tab on Reports built (customer cohorts).** Orders carry `t_order.customer_key`, a one-way hash of Shopify's customer id (migration 0031; no name, email or address). `GET /customers/ltv` groups customers by month of first order: customers, repurchase rate, revenue and profit per customer month by month (heat map), ad spend of the month, CAC and LTV:CAC; filters by period, country and source of the first order. Revenue per cohort adds up to the Overview's revenue (tested). Limits: cohorts only see the imported history (90 days at most), so the oldest month is unreliable; CAC is blended (all ad spend of the month over new customers); guest checkouts have no key and are counted apart; no CSV or PDF for LTV yet; no discount-code filter (not stored). The order query asks for `customer { id }` and falls back without it if Shopify refuses (protected customer data): **not yet verified against a real store**, and existing orders get a key only when imported again. `customers/redact` removes a customer's key from their orders. Privacy policy, retention table and the security test were updated.
- **Guided setup (non-blocking).** `SetupGuide` (opened from the setup card, and offered once on the first visit) walks through orders (importing in the background, no waiting), product costs, payment fees (one click saves 2.9% + $0.30, editable, dated a year back so it covers the imported history), shipping (one typical cost per order saves a flat rule) and the optional ad platforms and expenses. Every page can be skipped, closing it loses nothing, and rules saved there trigger a background recalculation (`fill_gaps`) so past orders pick them up. The 2.9% + $0.30 default is an estimate, labeled as such until Shopify reports real fees.
- **Four more ad platforms (Microsoft Ads, Snapchat, Pinterest, Reddit).** Sign-in, account choice, encrypted tokens, daily spend import and token renewal for each, in the same flow as Meta, Google and TikTok (migration 0032; `ad-oauth-more.ts`; importers in `apps/worker/src/ads`). Written from the platforms' public documentation and tested with fakes only; Connect stays off until the owner adds each platform's app credentials. Microsoft (SOAP report, zip, rotating refresh token) and Reddit are the most likely to need fixes on the first real account. Setup steps: `docs/operations/ad-platforms.md`.
- **Plan limits shown as locks.** The server already refused anything outside the plan; the screens now show it first (`usePlan`, `PlanLock`): a period reaching further back than the plan's history, and PDF, are locked in their dropdowns with a lock and the name of the plan that has them; the report Download button and the Add schedule button are disabled with a note when the month's files or the scheduled emails are used up; Connect on the ad platforms is disabled when the plan's ad accounts are all connected. Nothing is locked while the plan is unknown. Three more plan rules (draft, in `packages/contracts/src/plans.ts`): `ltvReport`, `carrierInvoices` and `bulkCostTools` (CSV import and bulk edit by vendor, product or collection) are off on Free and on from Growth; the server answers 403 plan_limit and the screens show the lock. Setting one product's cost, exporting costs and looking at imported invoices stay open on every plan. Bulk edit by a typed list of SKUs or variant ids is not refused by the server (it is the same call as one product's own edit); only the screen locks it. With billing off every shop has the Pro plan, so nothing is locked in development.
- **Four plans, and a comparison table (draft).** Free ($0, 50 orders), Basic ($9, 200 orders, 60 days, cost import, 1 scheduled email), Growth ($29, 750 orders, marked Most popular) and Pro ($79, 2,500 orders). Competitors' entry paid plans are about $35-49 (TrueProfit, BeProfit, Profit Calc), so Basic is a low-friction first step and Growth is the target. Plans screen: one table with a row per limit, a Monthly/Yearly switch (a year costs ten months, one annual Shopify charge in Billing API mode; with Shopify App Pricing the merchant picks on Shopify's page) and a 14 day free trial (`trialDays` in the Billing API subscription). Migration 0033 widens `s_subscription.plan`. All prices and limits are drafts (`approved: false`) until the owner approves them; Shopify plan names in the Partner Dashboard must be Basic, Growth and Pro.

- **Shipping screen and carrier invoices done.** A Shipping screen after Marketing holds the carriers and the shipping cost rules (moved from Settings). A carrier's invoice file (CSV: DHL, UPS, FedEx, Amazon Shipping or another) is imported, its rows are matched to orders by the order number on the label, and what the carrier charged becomes the actual shipping cost of those orders (status Actual, source "carrier invoice"), replacing the estimate of a rule. The merchant can choose the columns when the names are not recognised, an invoice can be taken back, and the same file cannot be imported twice. Tables `f_carrier_invoice` and `f_carrier_charge` (migrations 0029 and 0030). A live connection to a carrier account (API) is not built: the carriers' public APIs do not give the invoiced amount of parcels created elsewhere. Marketing and Shipping share one integrations list (`IntegrationList`). Shipping cost rules and payment fee rules are added and changed in a dialog (Add rule, Edit, Remove), like expense categories.
